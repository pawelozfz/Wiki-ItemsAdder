First, use iatag to check the item’s CustomModelData.
Hold the custom ItemsAdder item, then use iatag
<img width="656" height="213" alt="{1D916F3A-EE35-4A90-9ED4-57B884E10022}" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e5437f6a-ad72-44aa-97e9-a82f03e34c58" />
In the image, the float value of the item in hand is 95, and the item is a book.
Now go to your DecentHolograms and use:
/dh line set DUNGEON 1 1 #ICON: BOOK {CustomModelData:95}
